HOW IT SITS IN THE APP
No fifth tab. Today · Fixtures · Table · Clubs stays. News is a card on the board and a pushed screen behind it — reachable in one tap from the place people already open, and out of the way of the four things the app is for.
The card sits under the match. Board order is live/next match, then Around your clubs, then the rest of the round. Scores first, always: a headline is never the reason someone opened this app.
Three stories, then out. One with a picture, two as single lines, and a count of what is new since the last visit. The card is a doorway, not a feed.
Filters are the site's. All · your clubs · a league. Club-level filtering lives on the club page, where the reader already picked a club.
Tap opens the publisher. Third-party headlines open in a Safari view — the sheet says whose article it is before it loads, because the app is not the author. AltaGama's own pieces open in-app with no sheet.
NOTES FOR BUILD
Headlines are quotes. Publisher's language, never translated or trimmed — the same rule the news widget carries. Only the furniture follows the app's language.
Topic is optional. Every SPORT item files under none; the chip is simply absent and the publisher leads. Row three on the list is that case.
Age is relative and recomputed on appear, not stored as a string. Group headers are Today / Yesterday in the reader's zone.
Attribution is not a footnote. Publisher on every row, and the aggregation line at the end of the list.
Same feed as the widget — one endpoint, one cache. The widget's news.json writer can read this screen's query rather than fetch twice.
Empty states. Follows nothing → the follow prompt already used on the board. Follows clubs, feed quiet → say so, and keep the filters visible.
