1AltaGama FC iOS — design language + first directions
Where this starts
Identity carries. The web look doesn't.

Per your call and ADR 0014, only two things cross over from Cronogol: the mark and the lime. Everything else is built for iOS — a graphite ground instead of the web's navy, SF Pro at native type sizes instead of Chivo/Bricolage, and platform controls (tab bar, sheets, segmented, swipe actions) rather than web chrome.

System fonts also keep expo-font and typeface licensing out of v1 — the open question 0014 left.
