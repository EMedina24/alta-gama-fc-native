Squad builder, rebuilt for one thumb
The web builder is a drag-and-drop board with the squad in a column beside the pitch. Neither survives a phone: there is no room for a second column, and a finger dragging a name is a finger covering the slot it is aiming at. So placement is a tap, the squad lives on a rail under the pitch instead of in a modal, and everything that was a side panel is a sheet. Live prototype — tap a slot, tap a player, change the shape.

WHAT CHANGED FROM THE WEB BUILDER
Tap, don't drag. A dragged finger covers the slot it is aiming for, and a 46pt token is smaller than the hand moving it. Tap a slot, tap a player. Dragging still works for re-arranging — a placed token can be dragged onto a teammate to swap, which is the one gesture where the target is already visible.
The squad is a rail, not a modal. The web version's mobile fallback opens a sheet per placement — eleven sheets to fill a pitch. A horizontal rail under the pitch keeps the board visible while you fill it, and it filters itself to the line you tapped.
Placed players leave the rail. On the web they grey out in place; here the rail is short enough that removing them is the clearer signal, and the count chip carries progress.
Panels become sheets. Shape, look and export are three detented sheets instead of three side panels, each closing on Done — no chrome on the pitch itself.
One share path. The web page needs its own Instagram, Facebook and X buttons because the browser has no share target. iOS has one: render at 1080×1350 and hand it to the system sheet.
NATIVE NOTES FOR BUILD
Haptics carry the state change. Light impact on place, rigid on swap, warning on clear-pitch — the pitch is too small for an animation to read as confirmation.
Long-press a token for the context menu: Swap, Remove, Player page. Keeps three actions off the toolbar.
Clear pitch asks. Destructive confirmation in an action sheet — eleven taps are behind it.
The XI survives backgrounding. Persist formation, look and placements per club; a half-built lineup that vanishes is the reason people stop mid-build.
Squad data is the site's. Official league registrations, same caveat copy — a newly announced signing takes days to appear, and the app must say so where the rail ends, not silently omit him.
Export is off-screen. Draw the 1080×1350 card in code at full size, never a screenshot of a 361pt pitch scaled up.
