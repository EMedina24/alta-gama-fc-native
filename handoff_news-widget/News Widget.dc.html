WHAT THE EXTENSION CAN AND CANNOT DO
A widget process cannot fetch. Headlines and images are written into the App Group by the app first — widget/news.json plus news/{id}.jpg, downscaled to the sizes drawn here. Headlines stay in the publisher's language: they are quoted, not translated, so only the furniture follows the reader's locale. Topic is optional — a fifth of rows arrive with none, and the chip is simply absent. Age is the one value computed at render time, from the timeline entry's own date. One Link per row to the publisher. Full contract in NEWS-WIDGET.md.
