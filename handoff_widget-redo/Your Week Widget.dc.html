Day spine — one timeline, three stops
A week has one next match, not three. The tile runs a single lime-to-grey spine with three date stops: the next stop opens up to hold the kickoff, the other two collapse to a line each. Nothing nests, nothing truncates, and the eye travels one direction — down. Drawn at 2× a 338×158pt medium tile.

The date gutter is fixed at 48pt so the spine stays plumb whatever the labels say. Kickoff leads at 46px ultralight against the lime day — the two values a reader actually checks — with the side tag on the same baseline so it never crowds a club name.
Spine colour carries the state: solid lime node with a 4pt halo for the next match, hollow rings for the rest, and the rail itself fades lime→white 10% as it descends. The lime radial in the top corner is decoration only, never a data channel.
Payload: homeCrestSlot/awayCrestSlot per fixture, dayLabel + dateLabel + timeLabel, and the followed side. Crests are published for LaLiga only — anything else draws its abbreviation tile, never a generic crest. Smallest type on device is 7pt (the collapsed rows' date step).
Furniture (YOUR WEEK, 3 CLUBS, HOME) is pre-formatted in the snapshot's Copy struct, so the widget localises without .lproj.
